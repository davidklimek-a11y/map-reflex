Privacy Policy

Last updated: 15 September 2026

Map Reflex publishing tool ("the app") is a private tool used by the owner of the Map Reflex YouTube channel to upload, publish and measure that channel's own videos. It is not offered to anyone else.

What the app accesses

With the channel owner's permission, the app uses the YouTube Data API and the YouTube Analytics API to:

  • upload videos to the owner's channel and change their visibility;
  • read the channel's own video details, view counts and analytics.

It does not access any other Google account, and it does not collect data about viewers.

How data is stored and shared

Authorisation tokens and the channel's video statistics are stored only on the owner's own computer. They are not sold, not shared with third parties and not used for advertising.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Google and YouTube terms

The app uses YouTube API Services. See the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.
